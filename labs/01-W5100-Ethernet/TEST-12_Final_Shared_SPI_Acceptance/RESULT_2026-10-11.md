## Practical conclusion

The final acceptance test used only stock Arduino libraries (`SPI`, `SD`, `Ethernet`, `EthernetUDP`) and a replacement UNO. The SD baseline passed, including write and byte-for-byte read verification, but Ethernet DHCP failed immediately after the SD phase.

Across the completed investigation, shared-SPI behavior changed with board/card/power/startup state and was not reliable enough for engineering use. The issue was observed across more than one board configuration and with more than one microSD card. The exact electrical root cause was not isolated.

For project purposes, this is sufficient to classify the **second tested board/sample as defective or unsuitable in the onboard microSD/shared-SPI function**. The repeated failures indicate a high practical risk of clone-board schematic/manufacturing defects or inadequate SPI isolation. This is an engineering qualification for the tested hardware, not a claim that all W5100 shields have this defect.

## Lab decision — SD branch CLOSED

No further repair, rework, pull-up experiments, library forks, or attempts to force a PASS will be performed for the onboard microSD subsystem. This hardware is inexpensive experimental equipment, and the W5100 platform itself is legacy/outdated. Repair, rework, or development of custom compatibility layers is therefore not a project objective.

LAB-01 continues as **Ethernet-only**:

- W5100 Ethernet remains the supported subsystem.
- D4 (microSD CS) shall be held HIGH in Ethernet firmware.
- Future tests and applied projects may use DHCP, TCP, UDP, HTTP, GPIO control and other W5100 networking functions as needed.
- Onboard microSD is excluded from acceptance criteria and future project scope unless a new hardware platform is explicitly introduced.

Earlier Ethernet-only TEST-01 through TEST-07 remain valid within their documented scope.
