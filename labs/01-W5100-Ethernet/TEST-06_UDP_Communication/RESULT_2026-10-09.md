## Arduino serial verification

`UDP ECHO #1` through `UDP ECHO #24` appear in the user-provided UART log. Packet sizes: first 1, 16, 64, 128 bytes, then twenty packets of 32 bytes. Received peer address `192.168.1.65`; first logged client source port was `59444`, later entries `59443`. These are **remote client source ports reported by the UNO**, not the UDP listening port (5001). The reason for two different client source ports cannot be established solely from this serial excerpt, and no causation is claimed.
