## Scope, open evidence and non-claims

**FULL PASS for the agreed, bounded TEST-07 cable-recovery protocol.** This demonstrates successful reconnection and sustained echo response without a *requested* Arduino reboot; however, the PowerShell output alone **cannot independently prove that no spontaneous Arduino reset occurred**. To make a firmware-uptime claim, collect and compare Serial `STATUS uptime_s` across the event.

This test does not establish actual DHCP lease renewal, behavior under router failure, cable flap duration thresholds, service recovery time from physical insertion, load tolerance or long-term 24/7 reliability. Classic W5100 `linkStatus()` may be UNKNOWN.
