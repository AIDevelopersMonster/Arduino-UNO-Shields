# TEST-11 — Raw CMD0 probe result

**Date:** 2026-10-11  
**Result:** **INVALID BASELINE / NO CONCLUSION ABOUT LAN**

Observed immediately after sketch startup, before any LAN command:

```text
STATE lan=OFF D4=HIGH D10=HIGH MISO_PULLUP=LOW
C0
C0 MISO before=LOW ready=NO polls=8392 R1=0xFF after=LOW
C0 RAW CMD0: FAIL
```

A second baseline `C0` also failed. Therefore TEST-11 did not establish a valid pre-LAN SD response and cannot be used to infer that W5100 activity caused the raw SD failure.

The later `L1 DHCP: FAIL` occurred only after the invalid raw SD probe and is not treated as independent evidence about Ethernet.

The passive `MISO_PULLUP` LOW observation is also non-diagnostic on this shield: an AVR internal pull-up is too weak to prove whether the line is actively driven or biased by shield circuitry.

**Decision:** retire the hand-written raw-CMD0 probe. Replace it with the proven low-level `Sd2Card::init()` implementation from Arduino SD and report its native error code.
